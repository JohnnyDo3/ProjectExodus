🎨 Design System

A comprehensive guide to Project Exodus's living, breathing design language

Design Principles

The foundational philosophy guiding every design decision in Project Exodus

🌅

Living & Breathing

Our design adapts to natural rhythms. Colors shift throughout the day based on real solar times, creating a living interface that feels organic and connected to the natural world.

🌿

Earthy & Organic

We draw inspiration from nature with earthy color palettes, organic shapes, and flowing animations that feel grounded and sustainable.

💪

Bold & Confident

Thick borders (4px), heavy font weights (900), and high contrast demonstrate our commitment to making a real impact on sustainability.

🎮

Playful & Engaging

Learning about sustainability should be joyful. We use gamification, animations, and delightful micro-interactions to keep users engaged.

♿

Accessible to All

High contrast ratios, large touch targets (44px minimum), clear typography, and semantic HTML ensure everyone can participate.

🚀

Performance First

Smooth 60fps animations, optimized images, and efficient code delivery ensure a fast experience on all devices.
