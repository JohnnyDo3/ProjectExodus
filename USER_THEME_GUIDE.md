# User Theme Preference System

## Overview

Project Exodus now gives you **complete control** over your viewing experience! Choose from 6 beautiful themes or let the site automatically sync with the real sun.

## How to Change Your Theme

### Desktop
1. Look at the top-right of the page
2. Find the theme selector button (shows a sun, moon, or clock icon)
3. Click it to open the theme menu
4. Choose your favorite theme!

### Mobile
1. Tap the menu button (☰) in the top-right
2. Look for the theme selector near the top
3. Tap it to see all theme options
4. Pick the one you like!

## Available Themes

### 🌍 Auto (Follows Sun)
**"Changes with real sunrise and sunset"**

This magical theme watches where the sun is in YOUR part of the world and changes colors throughout the day! It's like having a window that shows what's happening outside.

- **Morning**: Warm, golden sunrise colors
- **Day**: Bright and sunny
- **Evening**: Beautiful sunset oranges and pinks
- **Night**: Dark sky with stars

Perfect for people who like natural rhythms and want the site to match the real world.

## Your Preference is Saved!

### For Everyone
Your theme choice is saved in your web browser, so it'll be there next time you visit!

### For Logged-In Users
Even better! When you're logged in, your theme preference is saved to your account. This means:

- ✅ Same theme on any device you use
- ✅ Same theme on any browser
- ✅ Your choice is remembered forever (until you change it)

## Tips

- **Try them all!** Click through each theme to see which one feels right
- **Match your mood**: Change themes based on how you're feeling
- **Match your environment**: Use Dark theme in dim rooms, Light in bright rooms
- **Let it flow**: Try Auto mode to experience smooth, natural color changes

## Questions?

**Q: Will my theme choice affect other people?**
A: No! Your theme is just for you. Everyone sees their own preferred theme.

**Q: Can I change themes anytime?**
A: Yes! Change as often as you like. It's instant!

**Q: What if I'm not logged in?**
A: No problem! Your theme is still saved in your browser.

**Q: Does Auto mode use my location?**
A: It asks for permission to know where you are so it can calculate when the sun rises and sets in your area. This makes the colors match the real sun! If you don't allow it, it uses default sunrise/sunset times.

## Privacy Note

Your theme preference is stored:
- Locally in your browser (for everyone)
- In your account settings (if logged in)

We never share your theme preference with anyone. It's just for making the site look the way YOU want!
